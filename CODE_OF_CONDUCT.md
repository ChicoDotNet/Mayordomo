# Code of Conduct

Mayordomo Engine is a technical project involving software engineering, deterministic simulation, game architecture, and collaboration across disciplines.

Participants must treat people with respect; critique code, architecture, behavior, evidence, and ideas rather than people; credit sources; protect confidential information; accept correction; and keep disagreement proportional.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, sexualized behavior, doxxing, deceptive impersonation, plagiarism, repeated disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not acceptable.

The project may support faith-oriented products without requiring contributors to share those beliefs. Technical channels must not be used to attack participants over religion or lack of religion.

Do not post secrets, proprietary Mayordomo content, private player/customer information, or sensitive security details in public channels.

Maintainers may use clarification, warnings, temporary restrictions, suspension, or permanent removal depending on severity and context.
