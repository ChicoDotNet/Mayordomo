## Repository split

The intended boundary is:

- `ChicoDotNet/Mayordomo` — reusable board-game engine and public-safe engineering assets;
- `ChicoDotNet/Mayordomo.Game` — private commercial Mayordomo implementation and Studio.

The private repository may contain Mayordomo-specific executable rules, official content, Studio workflows, licensed assets, branded clients, and commercial integrations.

## Public-safe history rule

Do not commit proprietary/private material here temporarily with the intention of deleting it before publication.

If this repository becomes public, earlier commits and branches may be reachable. Treat every commit as potentially public from the moment it is created.

## Trademarks and licensed products

Open-source licensing of this engine does not grant rights to the Mayordomo product identity or to any future licensed game/entertainment property.

Future licensed products must keep their rules/content/brand rights explicit and separate from the engine license.
